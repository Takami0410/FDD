Who We Are

MORE
THAN
A TEAM

Boundless FC is a futsal community built from a simple idea — that football is better when shared. Founded in 2022, we started as a small group of friends who just wanted a regular game. Today, we're a growing community of active members.

We run three open sessions every week, host our own tournaments, and compete in external competitions. Whether you're a seasoned player or just getting started, Boundless FC is your home.

The name says it all — we play without limits.
