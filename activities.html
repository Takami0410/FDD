Know Before You Play

RULES &
GUIDELINES

Session Registration +
  • All players must register before arrival — no walk-ins guaranteed a spot.
  • Registration opens 5 days before each session.
  • If you can't make it, cancel at least 24 hours in advance to free up your spot.
  • Repeated no-shows may result in temporary registration suspension.
On-Court Rules +
  • Standard FIFA futsal rules apply unless stated otherwise.
  • Sliding tackles are not permitted in casual sessions.
  • Goalkeepers must rotate every two games in open sessions.
  • All disputes are settled by the session coordinator — their call is final.
Equipment & Attire +
  • Futsal shoes or flat-soled trainers only — no studs or blades.
  • Shin guards are strongly recommended.
  • Bibs are provided on session day — no need to bring your own.
  • Members who have the official jersey are encouraged to wear it.
Tournament Format +
  • The Boundless Cup uses a standard single-elimination bracket.
  • Group stage (if applicable) determines seeding for the knockout round.
  • Teams must have a minimum of 5 players on match day — no forfeit exceptions.
  • External tournament eligibility requires a minimum of 4 sessions attended.
Code of Conduct +
  • Respect all players, coordinators, and venue staff at all times.
  • Any form of aggressive behaviour results in immediate removal from the session.
  • Boundless FC reserves the right to revoke membership for conduct violations.
  • Sportsmanship is non-negotiable — we win and lose together.
