Membership

JOINING
THE CLUB

How do I become a member of Boundless FC?+
Head to our Registration page and fill in the form with your details. Once submitted, our admin team will review your application and get in touch within 2–3 working days with confirmation and payment details.
Is there a membership fee?+
Yes. Membership is RM 30 per month, which covers access to all weekly sessions, discounted tournament entry, and eligibility for external league representation. Guest session rates apply for non-members.
Can I try a session before committing to membership?+
Absolutely. We welcome guests for up to two sessions at a guest rate of RM 15 per session. After that, we encourage you to register as a member to continue playing and access all benefits.
Is there a minimum age to join?+
Players must be 16 or older. Those aged 16–17 are required to submit a parental consent form before their first session. You can request this form via our Contact page.
How do I cancel or pause my membership?+
To cancel or pause your membership, reach out to us via the Contact page or WhatsApp with at least 7 days' notice before your next billing cycle. We're flexible — just let us know your situation.

Sessions

PLAYING
WITH US

When and where are sessions held?+
We run three sessions per week: Monday at 8PM, Wednesday at 9PM, and Friday at 10PM — all at Sports Planet, Shah Alam. Check the Activities page for any schedule updates.
How do I register for a session?+
Session registration is handled via our WhatsApp group for members. Spots open 5 days before each session on a first-come, first-served basis. We cap sessions at 20 players to ensure quality game time.
What should I bring to a session?+
Wear your Boundless FC jersey if you have one, or any comfortable sports attire. Futsal shoes or flat-soled trainers are required — no studs. Bring water, and shin guards if you prefer. Bibs are provided on the night.
What happens if I miss a session I registered for?+
We ask that you cancel at least 24 hours in advance so the spot can be given to someone else. Repeated no-shows without notice may result in a temporary registration hold to be fair to other members.
How are teams formed during sessions?+
Players are split into 4 balanced teams by the session coordinator, taking skill level and player familiarity into account. Teams rotate in a round-robin format so everyone gets consistent game time throughout the session.

Tournaments

COMPETING
& EVENTS

What is the Boundless Cup?+
The Boundless Cup is our annual in-house tournament where internal teams compete in a full single-elimination bracket. It's the highlight of the Boundless FC calendar — expect big energy, close games, and plenty of rivalry.
Can I enter the Boundless Cup as a solo player?+
The Boundless Cup operates on a team entry basis (minimum 5 players per team). If you don't have a full team, let us know and we'll try to match you with other solo entrants to form a composite team.
How do I get selected for external tournaments?+
External league and tournament spots are allocated based on attendance, performance, and attitude in weekly sessions. A minimum of 4 sessions attended is required for eligibility. Final selection is made by the coaching coordinator.
Are there fees to enter tournaments?+
The Boundless Cup has a team entry fee that is split between players. External tournaments vary by event. Members receive a subsidised rate from the club fund. Fees are communicated well in advance of each competition.

Payments

FEES &
BILLING

What payment methods do you accept?+
We accept payment via DuitNow QR, bank transfer, and cash (in person at sessions). All payments are confirmed via WhatsApp with a receipt. Please do not make payments to unofficial accounts.
When is the monthly membership fee due?+
Membership fees are due by the 5th of each month. Members will receive a reminder via the club WhatsApp group. Payment after the 10th without prior notice may result in temporary session access suspension.
Is there a refund policy?+
Membership fees are non-refundable once a session has been attended. If you have a valid reason (medical, emergency), contact us and we'll handle it on a case-by-case basis. We're a community, not a corporation.
What is the guest session rate?+
Guests pay RM 15 per session, which covers court rental and bibs. Guest slots are limited to a maximum of 4 per session to prioritise members. Payment is made on the day at the venue.

General

EVERYTHING
ELSE

How do I get the official Boundless FC jersey?+
Jersey orders are run in batches. When a new order window opens, it's announced via the club WhatsApp and on our News page. You can also enquire directly via the Contact page.
How do I follow Boundless FC on social media?+
Find us on Instagram at @boundless_fc. We post session highlights, tournament updates, and announcements regularly.
I have a question that isn't answered here — what do I do?+
Use our Contact page to send us an inquiry and we'll get back to you within 1–2 working days. You can also DM us on Instagram for a quicker response.
Can I bring a friend along to a session?+
Yes! Guests are welcome for up to two sessions. Just let the session coordinator know in advance so we can ensure there's a guest slot available. Guest spots are limited per session.
